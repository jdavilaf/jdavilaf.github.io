Cubo a Cubo

Privacy policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Data in the game

Cubo a Cubo does not require an account. JD Apps does not receive your gameplay progress or saved games. The app does not include advertising, tracking or gameplay analytics.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and in-progress puzzles, including undo history, on your device. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to any backups you manage through Apple.

Purchases and restoration

Apple handles payments and purchase restoration through the App Store. Cubo a Cubo checks with StoreKit which content your account can access. JD Apps does not receive your payment card details through the game.

Sharing challenges

If you choose to share a result, the app prepares a card with the challenge name, code and number of drops. You choose the recipients and the app used to send it.

Support email and deletion requests

If you email us, we use your email address and the information you choose to provide to respond. We retain support messages as needed to handle the request or meet applicable obligations. For privacy questions or deletion requests about correspondence, contact support@playjdapps.com.
