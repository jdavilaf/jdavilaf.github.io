Cubo a Cubo

Support

How do I play?

Open the question-mark button on Home or during a puzzle to practice. Choose a piece, rotate it, then use the position grid to move it. The colored outline shows where it will land. Use Layers to check gaps inside the board.

How do I resume a puzzle?

Choose Continue from Home. The app saves your board and undo history for each level. Undo takes back the last drop. Restart starts that puzzle over.

How do I open a shared challenge?

Open the challenge link on an iPhone with Cubo a Cubo installed, or open Levels, choose Enter code and use the code printed on the card. The current free challenge is also available from Home.

How do I restore the full campaign?

Open Levels and choose Restore Purchases near the bottom. Use the Apple Account that originally bought the campaign, and connect to the internet for restoration.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Cubo a Cubo is designed for iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The game uses Spanish if your primary language is Spanish and English otherwise.
