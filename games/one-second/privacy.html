Privacy Policy

This policy describes how information may be processed when you play ONE SECOND, an iOS game published by JD Apps.

Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Who operates the game?

JD Apps publishes ONE SECOND. For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@playjdapps.com.

2. Game data and device information

ONE SECOND may store gameplay settings and progress on your device. Depending on the features and services enabled, technical information such as device identifiers, app activity, and diagnostic data may be processed by third-party services described below. The exact data collected depends on your settings and the services active in the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

ONE SECOND may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may process device identifiers, IP address, advertising interaction data, and other technical information to deliver, measure, protect, and, where permitted, personalize advertising. Applicable choices and permissions, including Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission where required, govern certain uses.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy ↗.

4. Apple Game Center

If you use Game Center features, Apple may process your Game Center account information, achievements, scores, and related gameplay information according to Apple's policies and your settings. JD Apps does not need your Apple ID password to provide support.

Learn more: Apple Privacy Policy ↗.

5. Purchases and payments

If in-app purchases are available, transactions are handled by Apple. JD Apps does not receive your full payment card details. Purchase-related information may be made available to the developer to deliver or support purchased features.

6. Contact and support

When you email us, we receive the information you choose to provide, including your email address and message. We use it to respond to your request and address the reported issue. Please do not include sensitive personal information in support messages.

7. Retention, rights, and choices

You can manage applicable privacy permissions in iOS Settings, including tracking permissions. You can also contact us to ask about personal information we hold through direct support correspondence or to request its deletion, subject to applicable legal requirements. Data processed independently by Apple or Google is governed by their respective privacy controls and policies.

8. Children and international users

ONE SECOND is intended for the audience indicated in its App Store age rating. Privacy requirements may differ depending on the player's location and age. Parents and guardians can use Apple's parental controls to manage device and app permissions.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The latest version will be published at this URL, with an updated revision date.
