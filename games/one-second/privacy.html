Your information

Privacy policy.

Last updated: October 20, 2026 · English

1. Who is responsible?

Jorge Dávila is the data controller responsible for the processing described in this policy, operating under the public brand ONE SECOND. Privacy enquiries: support@playjdapps.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The game stores scores, daily challenge records, gameplay statistics, achievement progress, preferences and advertising frequency information locally on your device. The developer does not operate a separate player account system or gameplay database. Local information is retained until reset through available controls or removed with the app, subject to any device backups managed by Apple.

3. Game Center and purchases

If you use Game Center, scores and achievement progress are sent to Apple for leaderboards and achievements associated with your Game Center account. Public visibility depends on Apple's services and your account settings. Remove Ads purchases and restoration are processed by Apple. The app checks verified purchase entitlements; the developer does not receive your payment card details.

4. Advertising and consent

The free version integrates Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). The integration requests non-personalized ads, but this does not mean that advertising is free of personal-data processing. Depending on the service, configuration and permissions, Google may process network information, device or app identifiers, diagnostics, performance information, advertising interactions and privacy choices. Network information can be used to infer approximate location.

These services support ad delivery, measurement, service reliability and protection against invalid traffic. Advertising requests are subject to the privacy signals and permissions determined by UMP. Where required, a consent message lets you make choices before ad requests begin. You can revisit choices through Settings → PRIVACY CHOICES when the option is required and available. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already lawfully performed.

A verified Remove Ads purchase stops future ad requests and presentations in the game. Privacy settings may still contact Google's consent service. Previously processed information is subject to the provider's retention rules.

Learn more from Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps using its services.

5. Support enquiries

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use these to answer your request and investigate the reported issue. Messages are retained only as long as needed for support, related follow-up or applicable legal obligations. Email is handled through our professional email provider, Arsys. Please avoid sending sensitive information.

6. Purposes and legal grounds

We use information needed to provide requested game features and purchase support to perform the service you request. Consent applies to advertising-related storage, access or processing where required by law. Handling support enquiries, maintaining service reliability and preventing abuse may rely on legitimate interests, balanced against your rights. Certain records may be retained to meet legal obligations. Advertising-provider processing also depends on your choices and the grounds explained in the consent message.

7. Service providers and international processing

Apple provides App Store, StoreKit and Game Center services. Google provides advertising and consent services. Arsys provides professional email services. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process connection information such as IP addresses and request logs to serve and secure the site. These companies may process information outside your country under their applicable safeguards and privacy terms. See Apple’s Privacy Policy and GitHub’s Privacy Statement. No separate analytics scripts, advertising scripts or contact forms are installed on these support pages.

8. Retention and your choices

You can remove local app information by deleting the app, subject to device backups. Apple and Google manage retention for their own services according to their policies and your account controls. The developer cannot directly erase provider-held records through a local game reset. Contact us if you need help identifying the appropriate request route.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location and the processing involved, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal information, restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy, and withdraw consent. Contact the email above to make a request. We may need proportionate information to verify it. You may also complain to your local data protection authority, including the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

10. Children

The game is not specifically directed to children. We do not intentionally request children's personal information through support. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can assess the request and take appropriate action.

11. Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and show the revised date. For privacy questions, contact support@playjdapps.com.
