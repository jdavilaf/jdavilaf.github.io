Frequently asked questions

How does ONE SECOND work?

ONE SECOND is a precision-based arcade game. Pay attention to the timing and on-screen instructions: one mistake ends the run.

How do I improve my score?

Focus on the current challenge, practice your timing, and try again. The game rewards precision and consistency.

Game Center isn't showing my scores. What can I do?

Check that you are signed in to Game Center in iPhone Settings, have an internet connection, and are using the latest version of the game. If the issue persists, contact us with your iOS version and a description of the problem.

An ad does not load or a reward is not granted.

Check your connection and try again later. Ad availability can vary. If an issue persists, tell us which screen you were on and what happened. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

How do I report a bug?

Email us with your iPhone model, iOS version, game version, the steps to reproduce the problem, and a screenshot if possible.
