Privacy policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Data in the game

PointCraft Tennis works offline and does not require an account. The game does not send personal data or gameplay data to JD Apps or third parties. It does not include advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

Information stored on your device

The game stores preferences, career progress, match records and saved tournament rounds on your device. Use Reset Progress in the game settings to reset progress. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to any backups you manage through Apple.

Support email

If you email us, JD Apps uses your email address and the details you choose to share to respond to your request. For privacy questions or requests about a support email, contact support@playjdapps.com.
