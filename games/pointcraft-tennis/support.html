Support

How do I hit a shot?

On the court, hold on the ball to charge power. Draw toward your target and release. Use the sliders button to choose direction and power with alternative controls.

How do I resume a tournament?

From the home screen, open Singles Tournament and choose Resume Match when a round is saved.

How do I change the language?

Open the gear menu and choose System, English or Spanish.

Contact support

Email support@playjdapps.com. Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, game version and the steps that reproduce the issue.
