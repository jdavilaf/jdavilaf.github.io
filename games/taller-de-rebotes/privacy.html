Bounce Workshop

Privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

Game data

Bounce Workshop stores progress and preferences on your iPhone. JD Apps does not receive this data through the game. The app requires no game account and includes no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Purchases

Apple handles payments and purchase restoration through the App Store. The game uses StoreKit to check whether the full campaign is unlocked. JD Apps does not receive your card details through the game.

Support and privacy requests

If you email JD Apps, we use your email address and the information you share to handle your request. For privacy questions or deletion requests about support correspondence, email support@playjdapps.com.
