Bounce Workshop

Support

How do I play?

Open Help from Home to try the tutorial. Draw a ramp, pull back the capsule and release it. Collect the sparks and guide the capsule to the receiver. Settings includes an alternative tap mode.

How do I resume a circuit?

Choose Continue campaign on Home. Use the Workshop map to see your progress and replay available circuits.

How do I restore the full campaign?

Open Workshop map → Unlock the full campaign → Restore purchase. Connect to the internet and use the Apple Account that bought the campaign. See Apple’s purchase support for billing and refunds.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Bounce Workshop is designed for iPhone with iOS 17 or later. It is available in English and Spanish.

Contact support

Email support@playjdapps.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, game version, circuit number and steps to reproduce the issue. Leave payment details out of your message.
